CONCLUSION

Both K-way merge using a Min Heap and pairwise merging successfully
merge the three sorted transaction lists into one sorted list.

The Min Heap method uses O(N log k) time and O(k) extra space.
Pairwise merging uses O(Nk) time in general and O(N) extra space.

Therefore, K-way merge using a Min Heap is the more suitable approach
when the number of sorted files increases because it is more efficient.
